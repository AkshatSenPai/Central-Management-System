The first maintenance month is a trial month (Clause 03), at the end of which either Party may end the engagement without notice. Thereafter this Agreement runs month to month and renews automatically each month on payment. Either Party may terminate by giving 30 (thirty) days’ written notice (email or WhatsApp is sufficient).
